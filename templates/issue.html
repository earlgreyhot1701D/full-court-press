How this is made

The stats are counted from the play-by-play by code, never by the AI. Each team’s points are checked against the final score before anything prints. The voice is AI, and every number and name it writes is checked against those same facts. Anything that fails gets cut.

The name is a hand-me-down from an old courthouse newsletter. Unofficial fan zine.
